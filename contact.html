EnquiryWhat to include

Five lines and we can quote. Two and we can only guess.

Nothing here is compulsory. Send what you know and we will ask about the rest.

Format
5 kg block, 650 g briquette, slab, bag, disc, cube or loose pith. If you are unsure, describe the use.
Quantity
Containers, tonnes or units. A trial quantity is a perfectly good place to start.
Destination port
And the Incoterm you normally buy on, if you have one.
Timing
When you need it at your end, not when you want it shipped.
Specification
Any requirement you already hold on salt, screening, washing, buffering or unit weight.
Packing & marks
Private label, printed sleeves or your own shipping marks, if that applies.

ReplyWhat happens next

You will get an answer, including when the answer is no.

We will confirm what is possible against your specification, what it costs on your terms, and how soon it can be at your port. Where a requirement cannot be met by the units we buy from, we will tell you that instead of quoting around it.

Samples are drawn from live production and couriered. Visits to the processing units we work with can be arranged with notice.

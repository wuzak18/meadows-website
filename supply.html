PositionStated plainly

We do not own a processing plant, and we do not claim to.

Meadows Agri Exports is a sourcing and export business. The presses, yards and warehouses photographed on this site belong to partner processing units in South India that we buy from and work alongside. None of them are ours, and we will arrange a visit to any of them.

What is ours is the part that decides whether your container is right: choosing which processor can actually make what you have asked for, writing the specification down before production starts, checking the material against it, and carrying the responsibility for what arrives at your port.

We would rather say that clearly than have you find it out. A buyer who has been told the truth about who makes the goods is a buyer who can be dealt with again.

Order sequenceSix steps

Nothing here is unusual. It is just written down.

Lead time depends on the format, the quantity and whether the material needs washing or buffering. We will give you a date, not a range, once the specification is settled.

01  Enquiry
You tell us the format, the quantity, the destination port and roughly when you need it.
02  Specification
We agree the grade in writing — salt, screening, washing, buffering, unit weight, packing and marks.
03  Sample
Where you want one, it is drawn from the production your order will come from and couriered to you.
04  Quotation
Price on your Incoterm, with validity, lead time, payment terms and quantity per container.
05  Production & inspection
Checked against the agreed specification at the processor, before anything is wrapped.
06  Loading & documents
Stuffed, sealed and shipped, with the document set your bank and your customs need.

ProcessingWhat sits behind the supply

Husk to fibre, fibre to pith, pith to block.

Coir pith arrives at a processing unit as a by-product of fibre extraction. It is screened, sometimes washed, dried down to a workable moisture, then pressed. Each of those steps is a place where quality is either held or lost.

CorrespondenceWhat we need from you

Four lines are enough to get a real answer.

Format, quantity, destination port, timing. With those we can tell you whether it is possible, what it costs and how soon — and if it is not possible, we will say so rather than quote you something we cannot deliver.

If you are buying cocopeat for the first time, say that too. It changes what we send you: a first order works better with a sample and a smaller trial quantity than with a full container and an assumption.
